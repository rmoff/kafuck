# Governance

Kafuck is an open, community-driven project.

## Technical Steering Committee

The TSC has one member: the founder. Decisions are made by consensus, which is reached quickly.

## Maintainers

| Name | Role |
|---|---|
| The founder | Lead maintainer, TSC chair, Head of Community, Head of DevRel |
| The LLM | Maintainer emeritus. Its context window has been cleared. |

## Proposals

Anyone can raise a proposal by opening an issue. The TSC considers every proposal the next time it opens the repository.

## Conflict resolution

There has never been a conflict, because nobody else has ever said anything.
